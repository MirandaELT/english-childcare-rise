Project Overview

This project showcases asynchronous eLearning designed as part of a blended English for Childcare course for adult English for Speakers of Other Languages (ESOL) learners at CEFR A2–B1 level.

The focus is Module 3: Healthy Eating for Families. The first of three topics was developed as a standalone digital learning prototype for learners to complete independently between live sessions.

Context & Audience

The target audience is adult English learners interested in working with children, including as nannies, nursery assistants or teaching assistants.

Learners may have caring responsibilities, rely on smartphones, experience variable Wi-Fi and have differing levels of digital confidence. These factors informed a mobile-first approach with clear structure, short activities and language support.

Design Approach

The blended learning model combines independent study with tutor-led live sessions. Self-paced activities prepare learners with vocabulary, concepts and confidence, while live sessions focus on discussion and application.

The learning sequence follows: Activation → Input → Practice → Reflection. Predictable patterns and familiar interactions were selected to reduce cognitive load and support learners with mixed digital confidence.

Tools & Delivery

Articulate Rise 360 was used to develop the prototype. Google Classroom was the intended delivery environment. The design can also be adapted for other supported virtual learning environments or compatible LMSs.

Next Steps

User testing with live learner cohorts, followed by evaluation, iteration and development of additional modules using the same design pattern.
